# Capture Signal Patterns

> [!note]
> These are reusable signals, not facts about one pursuit. Capture them globally, then tag a workspace only after the pattern actually shows up there.

## Patterns worth logging

- Repeated government phrases across draft docs and Q and A usually mean ghost language is forming.
- New attachments without major scope rewrites often mean the buyer is exposing hidden workload or evaluation assumptions.
- Sudden silence from a once-responsive customer usually raises the value of competitor and incumbent reads.
- Teaming churn near the gate review often signals unresolved scope ownership or price pressure.

## What to record

1. The signal itself.
2. Why it matters.
3. What artifact would confirm or disconfirm it.
4. The next action owner.
