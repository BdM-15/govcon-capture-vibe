# Customer Intel Refresh Patterns

> [!note]
> Use this page when Intel Desk feels stale but the team cannot explain exactly why.

## Refresh triggers

- New leadership, reorganization, or mission memo changes the buyer story.
- Budget turbulence shows up in public signals before the acquisition team says it plainly.
- The solicitation widens scope language without widening schedule or funding language.
- The customer starts emphasizing transition, execution speed, or risk control more than feature depth.

## Minimum follow-up

- Pull one fresh public source.
- Restate the mission pressure in current language.
- Note whether the shift changes discriminator or staffing posture.
- Decide whether the intel belongs only in global notes or also in a workspace tag trail.
