# Proposal Recovery Patterns

> [!note]
> Capture these patterns once, then reuse them across pursuits before they become review comments.

## Common drift signals

- Features are named repeatedly, but evaluator benefit is still implied.
- The same proof point is doing too much work across several discriminators.
- Compliance coverage exists, but the narrative still hides the scoreable takeaway.
- Reviewers keep asking for a story the outline was supposed to make obvious.

## Recovery moves

1. Rewrite the section objective in evaluator language.
2. Force every major claim to name its proof.
3. Collapse duplicate discriminators.
4. Push unresolved issues into the right review lane instead of leaving them in prose.
