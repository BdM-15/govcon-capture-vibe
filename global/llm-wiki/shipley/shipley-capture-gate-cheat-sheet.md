# Shipley Capture Gate Cheat Sheet

> [!note]
> Use this page as a fast gate-readiness screen. If two or more answers are weak, capture work is still ahead of proposal writing.

## Core checks

1. Customer: can we state the mission pressure in one sentence without quoting the RFP back to the buyer?
2. Competition: do we know the likely incumbent edge, where it is soft, and what proof can overturn it?
3. Win path: are the discriminators tied to evaluation behavior, not just product features?
4. Proof: can every major claim point to a contract, metric, resume, or customer outcome?
5. Action bias: is there a named next move for customer contact, teaming, price, and solutioning?

## Evidence to carry forward

- Hot buttons stated in customer language.
- Ghost language candidates copied into a working note, then normalized.
- Proof points mapped to likely evaluators and likely skepticism.
- Open risks parked for the next [[pink-team-review-template]].

## Fast failure signs

- The team is debating format while still unclear on customer pain.
- Pricing is treated as a later problem even though labor mix is already implied.
- The incumbent story is generic rather than contract-specific.
- Reviewers cannot explain why we win without reading draft prose.
