# Red Team Readiness Template

> [!note]
> Use this when the draft is mature enough that reviewer time should focus on scoring risk, not brainstorming.

## Readiness check

1. Can an executive reviewer state the bid story after a five-minute skim?
2. Does every theme have proof the customer will trust?
3. Are compliance responses easy to verify without hunting through the volume?
4. Is staffing logic consistent with workload, price, and management claims?
5. Are obvious competitor counters addressed directly?

## Reviewer prompts

- Where does the draft overclaim?
- Which section reads like boilerplate?
- What would make a weakness likely?
- Which proof point is strongest, and which is still thin?
- What would a price or staffing reviewer challenge first?

- Strengths to preserve:
- Weakness risks:
- Material gaps:
- Executive-summary rewrite needed:
- Final owner / due date:
