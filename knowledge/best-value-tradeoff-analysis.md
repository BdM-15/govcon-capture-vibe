> **Entity type:** `concept`

Best value tradeoff procurements allow government to pay more for proposals offering higher non-price value. Tradeoff analysis determines whether technical/management superiority justifies price premium over lower-priced offers. Strategy implications: (1) Emphasize discriminators justifying premium, (2) Quantify risk reduction and value-added benefits, (3) Ensure discriminators align with stated evaluation factors, (4) Price must be 'fair and reasonable' but need not be lowest. Contrast with LPTA where lowest compliant price wins.
