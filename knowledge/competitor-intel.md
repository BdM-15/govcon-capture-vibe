# Competitor Intel

This topic holds intelligence on competitors bidding against us on active pursuits.

## How to use this tier

- Create a note per competitor (e.g. "SAIC AFCAP6 Profile")
- Tag with the pursuit slug so it groups under Pursuits as well
- Use the `competitive-intel` skill to pull USAspending obligation history
- Promote strong competitor assessments to `evergreen` status so they persist across pursuits

## Key questions per competitor

1. What is their incumbent footprint on this contract or NAICS?
2. What is their burn rate on similar IDIQs?
3. Who are their key personnel (LinkedIn, SAM.gov)?
4. What is their pricing strategy (T&M vs FFP vs CPFF)?
5. Ghost language in the RFP — did they write the PWS?
