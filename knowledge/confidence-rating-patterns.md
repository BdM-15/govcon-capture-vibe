> **Entity type:** `concept`

Performance risk assessment uses confidence ratings indicating evaluator's belief in offeror's ability to perform. Scale: High Confidence (no doubt offeror can perform), Significant Confidence (little doubt), Satisfactory Confidence (some doubt but acceptable risk), Limited Confidence (substantial doubt, risk mitigation needed), No Confidence (extreme doubt, likely failure). Past performance and management approach primarily drive confidence ratings. Proposal strategy: Provide proof points reducing evaluator doubt about execution capability. Relevant past performance is strongest confidence builder.
