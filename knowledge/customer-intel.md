# Customer Intel

This topic holds intelligence on agency customers — contracting offices, program offices, KOs, CORs, and end-users.

## What to capture here

- Customer hot buttons and discriminators surfaced in pre-solicitation Q&A or industry days
- Budget cycles, continuing resolutions, and likely award timelines
- Key personnel: KO, CO, COTR, Program Manager, end-user SPOCs
- Past performance impressions — what did they like / dislike about the incumbent?
- Relationship heat map: who on our team has a relationship with who?

## Sources

- SAM.gov synopsis pages and amendments
- Beta.SAM.gov procurement forecast
- FPDS-NG award history
- Industry day notes and Q&A transcripts (upload to Theseus for extraction)
- LinkedIn / conference contacts

## Customer Intel notes convention

Title format: `<Agency> <Office> — <Year> Profile`
Example: `HQ AMC/A4 — FY25 Customer Profile`
