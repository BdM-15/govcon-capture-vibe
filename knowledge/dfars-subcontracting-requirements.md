> **Entity type:** `requirement`

DFARS and FAR subcontracting requirements for contracts exceeding thresholds. Key requirements: (1) Small Business Subcontracting Plan (FAR 52.219-9) with specific goals by category (SB, SDB, WOSB, HUBZone, SDVOSB), (2) Limitations on subcontracting (FAR 52.219-14) for set-aside contracts, (3) Consent to subcontract for certain subcontract types. Proposal must include realistic, achievable subcontracting goals with named subcontractors where possible. Small business participation often scored in evaluation.
