> **Entity type:** `concept`

Deficiencies are material failures to meet requirements that are not correctable or would require major proposal revision. A single deficiency typically results in 'Unacceptable' rating for that factor. Types of deficiencies: (1) Non-responsive - fails to address requirement, (2) Non-compliant - violates stated requirement, (3) Material misrepresentation - false or misleading information. Deficiencies usually cannot be discussed during negotiations without creating unfair advantage. Prevention: Rigorous compliance review at Pink Team ensuring every requirement is addressed. Red Team should flag any potential deficiencies for correction.
