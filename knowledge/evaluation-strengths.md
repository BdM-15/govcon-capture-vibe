> **Entity type:** `concept`

Strengths are proposal aspects that exceed requirements or increase confidence in successful performance. Evaluators document strengths with specific citations to proposal content. Types: Significant Strengths (appreciably exceed requirements with substantial benefit), Strengths (exceed requirements or offer some benefit). Strategy for earning strengths: (1) Exceed stated requirements with quantified benefits, (2) Offer innovative approaches reducing risk, (3) Provide exceptional proof points from past performance, (4) Demonstrate deep customer understanding. Strengths justify higher adjectival ratings and price premiums in tradeoff.
