> **Entity type:** `concept`

Understanding government evaluator mindset improves proposal effectiveness. Evaluator reality: (1) They have many proposals to review under time pressure, (2) They score based on criteria - if your strength isn't in criteria, it won't help, (3) They need easy-to-find evidence - buried proof points get missed, (4) They document findings with proposal citations - make their job easy, (5) They compare across proposals - relative strength matters, (6) They are risk-averse - unsubstantiated claims raise concerns. Write to help evaluators find your strengths quickly. Use clear headings, call-out boxes, and explicit criteria mapping. Don't make them hunt for compliance.
