> **Entity type:** `strategic_theme`

Technique for neutralizing competitor strengths without naming them. Ghosting raises evaluator concerns about competitors through positive self-positioning: 'Unlike approaches that require lengthy transition periods, our proven team delivers Day 1 capability.' Effective ghosting is factual, professional, and highlights discriminators. Identified through Black Hat analysis. Never disparage competitors directly - focus on our superior approach to customer hot buttons.
