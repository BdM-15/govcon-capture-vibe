> **Entity type:** `concept`

Strategy distinctions for IDIQ task order competitions vs. standalone contracts. IDIQ advantages: Faster procurement (pre-qualified), smaller competition pool (only contract holders). Task order strategies: (1) Win the umbrella IDIQ first - you can't compete for TOs without it, (2) Track upcoming TOs through forecasts and customer engagement, (3) Short response times demand ready-to-go content library, (4) Build relationships across ordering agencies, (5) Accumulate task order performance for umbrella recompete. Fair Opportunity requirements still apply - don't assume easy wins. Many agencies have 'go-to' contractors on their vehicles based on prior TO performance.
