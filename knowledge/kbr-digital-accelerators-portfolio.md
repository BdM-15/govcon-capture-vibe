KBR's portfolio of proprietary digital products and platforms, organized into six capability domains: Digital Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, Autonomous Systems, and Enterprise Technology. KBR publicly states the portfolio supports 100+ active client initiatives across defense, national security, space, energy, and industrial sectors. These named products are the most defensible discriminators in proposals because they are owned IP with real deployments, not generic claims of 'AI capability' or 'cloud expertise.'
