> **Entity type:** `concept`

Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA) awards contract to lowest-priced offeror meeting minimum technical standards. No tradeoff between price and technical quality - either you meet the bar or you don't. LPTA appropriate when: requirements are well-defined, risk is minimal, and little value in exceeding requirements. Strategy: (1) Focus on compliance - meet but don't exceed, (2) Price to win - lowest compliant price wins, (3) Eliminate discriminators that add cost without evaluation benefit, (4) Ensure no weaknesses/deficiencies that would render proposal unacceptable. Innovation unrewarded in LPTA.
