> **Entity type:** `evaluation_factor`

Management Approach evaluates organizational capability to execute contract. Common subfactors: Program management (structure, reporting, governance), Quality assurance (QC/QA processes, continuous improvement), Risk management (identification, mitigation, monitoring), Transition planning (mobilization, knowledge transfer). Outstanding ratings require: detailed management plans with proven processes, organizational charts with named personnel, risk registers with quantified mitigation, realistic schedules with dependencies. Often evaluated alongside Technical Approach or as separate factor (15-25%).

## Attributes
- **weight**: 15-25%
- **importance**: Demonstrates execution capability
