# MS1 Qualification

## Decision

**Should we qualify this opportunity and assign a Capture Manager?**

MS1 is the first formal go/no-go gate. Leadership reviews the early
intelligence and decides whether the opportunity is real, fits the
business, and warrants the investment of a dedicated Capture Manager
plus initial Bid & Proposal funding.

## Timing

12-24 months before the Final RFP (FRFP) date.

## Briefer

Capture Manager (if pre-assigned), Account Executive / Account Manager,
or Operations Lead.

## What MS1 must establish

- Market intelligence has been gathered and the requirement is real
- Strategic alignment with the business unit / division has been
  confirmed
- Customer engagement plan exists (calls, site visits, RFI responses,
  white papers, demonstrations)
- Early solutioning has begun (capability gaps identified, possible
  responses scoped)
- Past performance baseline assessed for relevance
- Initial teaming conversations underway (large-business partners,
  small-business partners, subject-matter experts)
- Potential investments scoped (recruiting, facility, capital,
  internal R&D)
- Business unit has committed (named owner accountable)
- Initial Bid & Proposal funding estimate prepared

Once MS1 is approved, the Capture Manager is formally assigned. The
CM assesses the anticipated opportunity size against the threshold
of authority and determines the level of executive review required
as the opportunity matures.

## Promotion criteria (operational checklist)

These nine criteria are the MS1 gate scorecard. They are tracked
per-pursuit in `<pursuit>/05_reviews/gate_scorecards.json` under
`gate_slug: ms1-qualification`:

1. **Strategic fit confirmed** - opportunity aligns with stated BU /
   division strategy
2. **Customer decision-maker identified** - true Source Selection
   Authority known by name and role
3. **Viable customer access plan established** - documented path to
   reach SSA, Program Manager, Contracting Officer, technical
   evaluators
4. **Customer funding & opportunity credibility validated** - funding
   line exists or is forecast; opportunity is not vaporware
5. **Anticipated acquisition timeline defined** - draft RFP, FRFP,
   award, start, period of performance, option periods
6. **Customer's potential acquisition strategy understood** -
   best-value tradeoff vs LPTA, factor weighting, set-aside
   posture, contract type
7. **Business unit identified and accountable** - named owner with
   P&L responsibility
8. **Capture Manager available and qualified** - named, available,
   experienced for opportunity size and complexity
9. **OCI sweep initiated** - organizational conflict-of-interest
   assessment underway

## Briefing must cover (scoped for MS1)

- **Opportunity synopsis** - all known facts at this stage
- **BLUF** - strategic-fit context; "what will it take to win"
  emphasizes market intel, customer relationship maturity, talent
  identification, and need for external proposal-writing resources
- **Team assignments** - CM, Ops Lead, Solution Architect typically
  named at MS1
- **Evaluation methodology** - based on prior similar RFPs or
  inferred from customer history
- **SWOT** - early read
- **Path to Blue** - all 5 pursuit areas typically Low-to-Medium
- **Pricing strategy** - usually deferred or rough-order-of-magnitude
- **High-risk elements** - early risk register
- **Action plan** - shaping activities and intel-collection tasks
- **Recommendation** - pursue, pursue with conditions, or drop

## Often omitted at MS1

For subcontract roles, non-competitive (sole-source), and accelerated
acquisitions (OTA, CSO, Phase III SBIR), several content slides are
typically deferred or skipped:

- Proposed pricing summary (no price exists)
- Execution business case model (can be sketched or deferred)
- Detailed pricing strategy (deferred to MS2)

## Approval result

- **Approved**: opportunity advances to capture; CM and initial team
  resourced; B&P budget released for the next phase.
- **Approved with conditions**: specific gaps must be closed by an
  agreed date or the opportunity drops back to qualification.
- **No-pursue**: opportunity exits the pipeline; rationale recorded
  for [[lessons-learned]].
