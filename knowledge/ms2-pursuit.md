# MS2 Pursuit / No-Pursuit

## Decision

**Should we continue investing capture resources and stand up the
proposal team?**

MS2 is the interim review of capture progress. Leadership evaluates
whether the capture work since MS1 has materially advanced the
opportunity and whether continued funding is justified. Approval at
MS2 typically authorizes assignment of a Proposal Manager and
formal kickoff of proposal-development activities.

## Timing

9-12 months before the Final RFP (FRFP) date.

## Briefer

Capture Manager.

## What MS2 must establish

- Comprehensive capture strategy and capture plan exist and have
  been validated
- RFP shaping strategies are active (industry day participation,
  draft RFP comments, white papers, RFI responses)
- Customer engagement strategy is executing (documented call plans,
  client visits, briefings)
- Pricing strategy is taking shape (initial PTW work, fee structure,
  wrap rates, customer-directed plugs identified)
- Key / critical personnel hiring plan is active
- Competitive landscape is well understood (Bidder Comparison Chart
  populated, gap analysis done, ghost strategy drafted)
- Teaming discussions have advanced (NDAs in place, draft teaming
  agreements, work-share targets defined)
- Unique investment requirements identified and scoped (facility,
  CapEx, internal R&D)
- OCI assessment is complete and documented
- Pwin has been reassessed with evidence-based rationale
- Capability exercises (sample tasks, demos) have been completed or
  scheduled to validate fit

Once MS2 is approved, the Proposal Manager is formally assigned and
proposal-development resources are committed.

## Promotion criteria (operational checklist)

These nine criteria are the MS2 gate scorecard, tracked under
`gate_slug: ms2-pursuit`:

1. **Capture strategy and plan validated** - documented, reviewed,
   and approved by leadership
2. **Teaming strategy validated** - large/small business partner mix
   addresses capability, past performance, SB-goal, and geographic
   gaps
3. **Teaming agreements ready for execution** - drafted, legal-
   reviewed, partner alignment confirmed
4. **Pursuit team members available and qualified** - all roles
   filled or in active recruiting
5. **Initial pricing strategy acceptable** - position vs competition
   defined; PTW first-pass complete; fee structure realistic
6. **OCI assessment complete and acceptable** - mitigations in place
   for any identified conflicts
7. **Pwin reassessed and matured** - updated with evidence from
   customer engagement and competitive intel
8. **Unique hiring and investment expenses approved** - leadership
   has signed off on capture-phase investments
9. **B&P budget estimate approved** - updated forecast for the
   proposal-development phase

## Briefing must cover (scoped for MS2)

- **Opportunity synopsis** - updated with any new dates, scope
  changes, customer signals
- **BLUF** - emphasizes direct customer/CO engagement, gap-closure
  approaches (key hires, new processes, facility investments),
  competitive landscape, teaming strategy, updated B&P request
- **Team assignments** - Proposal Manager, Proposal Coordinator,
  Contracts Lead, Technical Lead, Staffing/BOE Lead, Pricing Lead
  added at MS2
- **Evaluation methodology** - based on draft RFP if available,
  otherwise informed by prior solicitations and customer history
- **SWOT** - matured with capture-phase intel
- **Path to Blue** - target Medium status across all five pursuit
  areas
- **Pricing strategy** - position-vs-competition, customer pricing
  guidance, pricing variables in impact order
- **Execution business case model** - preliminary, with
  conservative + optimistic columns
- **High-risk elements** - proposal risks + execution risks +
  documented responses
- **Action plan** - close-out actions, owners, due dates
- **Recommendation** - pursue with proposal team, adjust, or drop

## Often omitted at MS2

- Final proposed pricing summary (not expected until MS3 at
  earliest)
- Detailed compliance-matrix review (proposal hasn't started)

## Approval result

- **Approved**: Proposal Manager assigned; proposal team stood up;
  proposal-phase B&P budget released.
- **Approved with conditions**: specific gaps must close before
  proposal kickoff.
- **No-pursue**: opportunity drops; capture artifacts archived to
  [[lessons-learned]].
