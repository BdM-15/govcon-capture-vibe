# MS3 Bid / No-Bid

## Decision

**Should we bid this proposal?**

MS3 is the formal bid/no-bid gate. Leadership reviews mature capture
intelligence against the released (or near-released) RFP and decides
whether to commit full proposal resources. By MS3, the capture plan
should have been executed; this review tests whether execution
produced enough advantage to win.

## Timing

At Draft RFP (DRFP) release, OR approximately 30 days before Final
RFP (FRFP) release if no DRFP is anticipated.

If MS3 is conducted at DRFP, an MS3 update review is also conducted
at FRFP to validate that the procurement strategy hasn't materially
changed and no significant deviations exist between DRFP and FRFP.

## Briefer

Capture Manager (with Proposal Manager and Operations Lead supporting).

## What MS3 must establish

- Capture Plan has been implemented (not just written) and the
  results documented
- Unique investment activities are advanced or completed (key hires
  on board, facility ready, R&D delivered)
- Pwin has been reassessed against actual RFP language
- Win strategy has been validated against released requirements
- Teaming strategy is final - all teaming agreements executed,
  subcontractor SOWs scoped
- Pricing strategy and Price-to-Win analysis are mature and approved
- Recruiting and staffing plan has been activated
- Strategy adjustments post-RFP-release have been worked through
  with PropMgr and Ops Lead (win strategy, teaming, pricing,
  recruiting/staffing)
- Execution risk register is current and accepted by Operations

## Promotion criteria (operational checklist)

These eight criteria are the MS3 gate scorecard, tracked under
`gate_slug: ms3-bid-no-bid`. The first criterion is a trigger
condition (one of two must be true to even hold MS3):

1. **Trigger condition met** - DRFP released, OR FRFP expected
   within 30 days with no DRFP anticipated
2. **Influence activities demonstrated success** - evidence the
   shaping work moved the procurement in our favor (changed
   requirements, customer awareness, factor weighting)
3. **Documented teaming strategy and subcontractor selection in
   place** - executed teaming agreements, defined work shares,
   integrated solution
4. **Win strategy validated** - validated against the released RFP;
   discriminators map to evaluation criteria
5. **OCI reassessed and still acceptable** - re-checked against
   actual RFP language; mitigations remain valid
6. **Pwin reassessed and matured** - evidence-based Pwin against
   the released RFP
7. **Pricing strategy and PTW approved** - reviewed and signed off
   by leadership
8. **Execution risks assessed and deemed acceptable** - Operations
   has formally accepted residual execution risk

## Briefing must cover (scoped for MS3)

- **Opportunity synopsis** - now reflecting actual RFP terms
- **BLUF** - progress/closure update from MS2 elements; activation
  of recruiting/staffing plan; any additional B&P request
- **Team assignments** - Program Manager added at MS3 (where
  applicable)
- **Evaluation methodology** - now based on actual Section M from
  the released RFP; relative importance of factors confirmed
- **SWOT** - validated against released requirements
- **Path to Blue** - target Good status across all five pursuit
  areas
- **Pricing strategy** - approach to competitive and compelling
  price; significant pricing risks/opportunities; PTW alignment
- **Proposed pricing summary** - preliminary price proposal where
  technical estimates and pricing have been developed; major cost
  elements by contract period; comparison to PTW
- **Execution business case model** - matured; revenue and operating
  margin under conservative + optimistic scenarios
- **High-risk elements** - proposal risks (page count, submission
  complexity, sub dependency, key personnel, mandatory-section
  compliance) and execution risks (transition timeline, cost,
  key-personnel availability, bid-vs-execution margin)
- **Action plan** - close-out actions for proposal phase
- **Recommendation** - bid, bid with conditions, or no-bid

## DRFP vs FRFP MS3 update

When MS3 is conducted at DRFP, the FRFP update specifically checks:

- Procurement strategy unchanged (factor weighting, set-aside, contract
  type, Section L instructions, Section M factors)
- No major deviations between DRFP and FRFP that would invalidate
  prior decisions
- Pricing strategy and PTW still hold against actual FRFP
- Submission requirements (page count, format, Q&A windows) confirmed
- Capture Manager + Proposal Manager + Pricing Lead align on
  any required adjustments

## Approval result

- **Approved (Bid)**: full proposal resources committed; submission
  is the default outcome unless MS4 raises a stop condition.
- **Approved with conditions**: specific gaps must close before
  proposal-development resources unlock.
- **No-bid**: opportunity exits; rationale recorded; team
  reassigned.
