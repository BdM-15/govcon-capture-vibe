# MS4 Pricing Approval

## Decision

**Approve the final price and authorize submission?**

MS4 is the final compliance, pricing, and execution-risk review.
Executive leadership confirms that a compelling and compliant
proposal has been developed (substantiated by successful color-team
reviews), that the bid price is acceptable on accuracy/realism/
reasonableness grounds, and that residual execution risks remain
acceptable. Approval at MS4 releases the proposal for submission.

## Timing

7-14 days prior to proposal submission.

## Briefer

Multi-discipline panel:

- **Proposal Manager** - confirms compliant and compelling
  proposal status, color-team review outcomes
- **Capture Manager** - presents the bid price (supported by
  Pricing Lead and Contracts Manager)
- **Pricing Lead** - bid-price details, PTW alignment
- **Contracts Manager** - solution / teaming / pricing strategy
  review
- **Legal POC** - terms-and-conditions, OCI, IP, risk-allocation
  review
- **Operations Lead** - accepted execution risks

## What MS4 must establish

- Proposal is compliant with every Section L instruction and Section M
  evaluation factor (verified via compliance matrix)
- Proposal is compelling - discriminators are explicit, quantified,
  customer-relevant; FAB chains hold; ghost language counters known
  competitor weaknesses; graphics carry the message; win themes are
  inserted at the Executive Summary and each volume opener
- Color-team reviews have completed successfully (Pink for compliance
  + structure, Red for persuasiveness + discriminators)
- Bid price is accurate (no math/scope errors), realistic
  (consistent with the proposed solution), and reasonable
  (defensible vs evaluated-price benchmarks and PTW)
- Solution / teaming / pricing strategies have been reviewed by
  Legal and Contracts
- Execution risks have been formally accepted by Operations
- Submission logistics are confirmed (portal upload tested, courier
  arranged if hardcopy, registrations current)

## Promotion criteria (operational checklist)

These three criteria are the MS4 gate scorecard, tracked under
`gate_slug: ms4-pricing-approval`:

1. **Compelling and compliant proposal developed** - proven by
   successful color-team reviews (Pink, Red, and any
   intermediate Orange/Black-Hat reviews)
2. **Pricing acceptable on accuracy, realism, and reasonableness**
   - bid price defensible to leadership and consistent with
   evaluation factors
3. **Execution risks continue to be acceptable** - Operations
   formally accepts residual execution risk

These are intentionally few but absolute. Each is a "must" - failure
of any one is a stop condition.

## Briefing must cover (scoped for MS4)

- **Opportunity synopsis** - final, reflecting any FRFP amendments
- **BLUF** - submission-readiness summary
- **Team assignments** - all roles confirmed; transition team
  identified for post-award
- **Evaluation methodology** - confirmed against final RFP, including
  any amendments
- **Path to Blue** - target Good-to-Complete status across all five
  pursuit areas
- **Pricing strategy** - final position vs competition; how the
  pricing approach defends accuracy, realism, reasonableness
- **Proposed pricing summary** - the final proposed price for
  approval; major cost elements by contract period; revenue and
  quotation analysis; comparison to PTW
- **Execution business case model** - final business-case model
  with revenue and operating-margin projections
- **High-risk elements** - any residual proposal risks (last
  amendments, submission logistics) and execution risks (transition,
  staffing, bid-vs-execution margin)
- **Action plan** - submission logistics, post-submission
  lessons-learned plan, transition activities
- **Recommendation** - submit, submit with executive conditions,
  or stand-down

## Stop conditions

Any of the following at MS4 should trigger a stand-down or major
intervention:

- Color-team reviews surfaced compliance gaps that haven't been
  closed
- Pricing analysis shows bid price falls outside the
  accuracy/realism/reasonableness band
- Operations cannot accept residual execution risk
- Legal or Contracts identifies an unresolved teaming, OCI, or
  T&C issue
- Submission logistics are not confirmed (portal access, hardcopy
  production, courier)

## Approval result

- **Approved**: proposal authorized for submission at the proposed
  price; submission team executes per logistics plan.
- **Approved with conditions**: specific changes required before
  submission (price adjustment, compliance fix, risk mitigation).
- **Stand-down**: do not submit; rationale recorded; opportunity
  archived for [[lessons-learned]].

## Post-submission

Independent of MS4 outcome, every submitted proposal triggers:

- Post-submission lessons-learned review (within 30 days, regardless
  of award outcome)
- Win-loss interview after award decision (regardless of outcome)
- Update to incumbent / competitor intelligence files
- Update to PTW database with actual price-vs-evaluation data
