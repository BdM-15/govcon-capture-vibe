> **Entity type:** `concept`

O&M staffing based on system complexity and availability requirements. Drivers: (1) Number of systems/applications supported, (2) Technology complexity, (3) Availability requirements (99.9% vs 99.99%), (4) Change volume (releases, patches, configurations), (5) Coverage model (follow-the-sun, on-call, 24×7). Rule of thumb: 1 FTE per 3-5 moderately complex systems for steady-state O&M. High-availability systems (99.99%+) require dedicated staff per system. Include application-specific SMEs, DBAs, and infrastructure support in mix.
