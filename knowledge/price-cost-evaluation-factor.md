> **Entity type:** `evaluation_factor`

Price/Cost evaluation assesses proposed pricing for reasonableness, realism, and balance. Not rated adjectivally - evaluated numerically and analyzed for: Price reasonableness (comparison to IGCE, other offers, market rates), Cost realism (for cost-reimbursement - can work be done for proposed cost?), Price balance (no front-loading or material unbalancing). For LPTA: lowest technically acceptable price wins. For tradeoff: price premium must be justified by technical superiority. Include credible Basis of Estimate (BOE) documentation. Unrealistically low prices raise performance risk concerns.

- **weight**: Varies by award type
