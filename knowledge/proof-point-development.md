> **Entity type:** `strategic_theme`

Proof points are concrete evidence supporting claims in win themes. Types: (1) Past performance examples - specific contracts demonstrating capability with quantified results, (2) Metrics and statistics - measurable achievements (e.g., '99.7% on-time delivery'), (3) Certifications and credentials - CMMI, ISO, professional certifications, (4) Customer testimonials - quotes or letters from satisfied customers, (5) Third-party validation - analyst reports, awards, industry recognition. Every claim needs supporting proof. Weak proof points: generic statements, unquantified claims, outdated examples. Strong proof points: recent, relevant, quantified, from similar customers.
