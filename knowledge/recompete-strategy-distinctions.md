> **Entity type:** `concept`

Strategic differences between competing as incumbent vs. challenger. As incumbent: (1) Emphasize continuity and mission knowledge, (2) Highlight performance record with specific metrics, (3) Propose retained staff for seamless transition, (4) Address any negative CPARS proactively with corrective actions, (5) Don't be complacent - many incumbents lose to hungrier challengers. As challenger: (1) Emphasize fresh perspective and innovation, (2) Ghost incumbent weaknesses subtly, (3) Recruit key incumbent staff for your team, (4) Provide detailed transition plan reducing risk, (5) Offer competitive pricing (incumbents often price high). Incumbents win ~60-70% of recompetes - advantage but not guarantee.
