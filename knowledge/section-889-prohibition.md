Section 889(a)(1)(A) and (a)(1)(B) of the FY2019 NDAA (Pub. L. 115-232), implemented via FAR 52.204-25, prohibits federal agencies from procuring or using covered telecommunications equipment or services from Huawei, ZTE, Hytera, Hikvision, Dahua, or their subsidiaries/affiliates — AND from contracting with entities that USE such equipment/services anywhere in the business. Proposal implications: offeror must represent (FAR 52.204-26) whether it does or does not use covered equipment; a 'does' answer without waiver is disqualifying. Flow-down required to subcontractors. Common trap: overlooked IP cameras, VoIP phones, or video-conferencing gear at a minor office location causes the entire representation to fail. Pre-bid supply chain audit is the only safe way to sign the rep.
