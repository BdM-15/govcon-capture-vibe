> **Entity type:** `concept`

High-level, bullet-form outline a contributor builds BEFORE prose drafting to ensure the customer requirement is answered directly. Distinct from the [[annotated-outline]] (proposal-wide blueprint) — Storyboard is per-section/per-author. Includes informative section headings (e.g., '2.5 Account Team Structure, Project Management, and Relationship Management (10 pages)'), planned graphics, themes to weave in, and proof points. Shipley Content Plan Template emphasizes flexibility — bullets and notes, not full sentences. Catches non-responsiveness early.
